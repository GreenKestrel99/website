Stock & POs by SkidRunner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

KP Automation / SkidRunner (“we”, “us”) operates the Stock & POs Shopify app (the “App”). This policy explains what data the App accesses, what it stores, and your choices.

What the App accesses

With your consent, the App requests these Shopify permissions:

  • read_products — to read products and variants for purchase orders and reorder rules.
  • read_inventory / write_inventory — to read stock levels and update quantities when you receive a purchase order.
  • read_locations — to associate purchase orders and reorder rules with your store locations.

The App requests no access to customer data and does not read orders, customer names, addresses, emails, or payment information.

What we store

  • Business records you create or import: supplier records (name, contact, lead times, MOQs, terms), purchase orders and their line items, reorder rules, and any data you import from Stocky. This is business operations data, not consumer personal information.
  • Session/authentication data required to keep the App connected to your store, managed by Shopify’s app framework.

How data is used

Your product, inventory, and location data is read to build purchase orders, generate purchase-order PDFs, and calculate reorder needs. Data is used solely to provide the App’s functionality and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Data retention & deletion

  • You can delete supplier records, purchase orders, and reorder rules at any time within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App triggers deletion of the App’s session data for your store.
  • We honor Shopify’s mandatory privacy webhooks:
    • customers/data_request and customers/redact — the App stores no customer personal data, so there is nothing to compile or delete.
    • shop/redact — the App deletes all stored data for your store (suppliers, purchase orders, reorder rules, imported records, and sessions).

Security

Data in transit is protected by HTTPS. The App uses Shopify’s authenticated session-token flow and verifies all webhooks by HMAC signature.
