KP Barcode Labels — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

KP Automation (“we”, “us”) operates the KP Barcode Labels Shopify app (the “App”). This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

What the App accesses

When you install the App on your Shopify store, with your consent it requests these Shopify permissions:

  • read_products — to read your products and variants (title, variant, SKU, barcode, price) so you can select them and print labels.
  • read_inventory — to optionally match label quantities to stock on hand.

The App requests no access to customer data and does not read orders, customer names, addresses, emails, or payment information.

What we store

  • Session/authentication data required to keep the App connected to your store (managed by Shopify’s app framework). This is deleted when you uninstall the App or on a shop/redact request.
  • The App does not store your product catalog, and it does not store any customer personal information.

How data is used

Product and inventory data is read on demand to generate the label PDF you request, and is used only to render that PDF. Generated PDFs are produced for your download and are not retained by us beyond what is needed to deliver the file to your browser.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data is processed solely to provide the App’s labeling function.

Data retention & deletion

  • Uninstalling the App triggers deletion of the App’s session data for your store.
  • We honor Shopify’s mandatory privacy webhooks:
    • customers/data_request — the App stores no customer personal data, so there is nothing to compile.
    • customers/redact — the App stores no customer personal data, so there is nothing to delete.
    • shop/redact — the App deletes any remaining session data for your store.

Security

Data in transit is protected by HTTPS. The App uses Shopify’s authenticated session-token flow and verifies all webhooks by HMAC signature.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change accordingly.
